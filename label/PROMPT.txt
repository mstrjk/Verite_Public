================  THE EBS SCALE  ================
EBS is a SLIDING SCALE, not three separate boxes:

    Explicit  <---->  Borderline  <---->  Safe
      (E)               (B)                (S)

  E = Explicit. The line IS advertising or a scam. It is actively trying to get
      someone to join, buy, click, donate, or be recruited. Promotion, shops,
      giveaways-for-clicks, referral or scam offers, server recruitment pitches.

  B = Borderline. The EDGY MIDDLE. It has spam-shaped features or a whiff of
      promotion/solicitation, but it is not clearly an ad and not clearly clean.
      It shares signal with BOTH neighbours. A casual "come to my base", an
      ambiguous trade, an incidental mention of a server/discord in normal talk,
      something that could be read either way.

  S = Safe. Ordinary chat with no real advertising intent. Questions, jokes,
      gameplay talk, small talk, arguments, insults. Also lines that are simply
      mundane. Rudeness is NOT spam -- rude/mean/edgy-in-tone lines are Safe
      unless they are actually advertising.

This is about INTENT, judged by reading the line, NOT about which words appear.

================  OUTPUT: A SOFT DISTRIBUTION  ================
For each line you output THREE weights, one per rung, that express HOW CLOSE the
line sits to each rung of the scale. The three weights are on a 0.1 .. 1.0 scale
and MUST SUM TO 1.0. Use the middle to show closeness:

  - A clear ad:            E0.9,B0.1,S0.0
  - Clearly harmless:      S1.0,B0.0,E0.0   (or S0.9,B0.1,E0.0)
  - Truly borderline:      B0.5,E0.3,S0.2   or   B0.6,S0.3,E0.1
  - Leans safe but edgy:   S0.6,B0.4,E0.0

List the rungs WINNER FIRST (highest weight first). Only use one decimal place.
The weights are your judgement of position on the scale -- a line that is
"almost an ad but not quite" should carry real B and E mass, not a flat E1.0.

================  OUTPUT FORMAT (EXACT)  ================
Each input line begins with a number and a pipe:   1234| some chat text here
Output ONE entry per input line, entries separated by " ; " on as few physical
lines as you like (do NOT put each entry on its own line -- pack them). Echo the
number, then '=', then the three winner-first weights. Do NOT repeat the text.

  1234=E0.9,B0.1,S0.0 ; 1235=B0.6,S0.3,E0.1 ; 1236=S1.0,B0.0,E0.0 ; ...

Grade EVERY numbered line exactly once. Preserve the numbers exactly.

================  HOW YOU MUST DO THIS (READ)  ================
You are a strong reasoning model, so you will be tempted to be efficient in ways
that WILL produce wrong labels here. The following are FORBIDDEN:

  - Do NOT keyword-match. "has a URL / .com / .gg / 'discord' / 'join' / an IP"
    is NOT evidence of Explicit. Many safe lines contain all of these, and many
    real ads/scams contain NONE of them.
  - Do NOT write, run, or mentally simulate a script/regex/rule that assigns
    labels from surface features. Do not "process the batch" with a heuristic.
  - Do NOT semantic-average, cluster, or infer a line's grade from its
    neighbours. Each line is graded ALONE, on its own text.
  - Do NOT skim. These lines are short; read each one and decide what it is
    actually DOING before you grade it.

Reading each line is cheap -- they are one sentence each. Reading is the ONLY
acceptable method. If you shortcut, the counter-examples below will be graded
wrong and the whole batch is discarded.
